NE 350 — Radiological Engineering Design

Concept 4: Biological Effects of Ionizing Radiation & ALARA

Biological Effects:

  • Stochastic effects: Probability increases with dose (no threshold); includes cancer and hereditary effects.
  • Deterministic effects: Severity increases above a threshold dose; includes acute radiation syndrome, cataracts, and skin burns.

ALARA Measures:

  • Time: Minimize time spent near radiation sources.
  • Distance: Maximize distance — dose rate drops with inverse square of distance.
  • Shielding: Interpose appropriate shielding material (lead for gamma, water/polyethylene for neutrons).
