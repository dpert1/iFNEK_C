NE 300 — Fundamentals of Nuclear Engineering

Concept 5: Purpose of the Moderator

Answer:

The moderator slows fast fission neutrons (~2 MeV) down to thermal energies (~0.025 eV) through elastic scattering collisions. This is necessary because fissile nuclides like U-235 have a much higher fission cross section (200x) at thermal energies, making a sustained chain reaction easier. Ideal moderators have low mass number (maximizing energy transfer per collision), low absorption cross section, and high scattering cross section. Common moderators include light water (H₂O), heavy water (D₂O), and graphite.
