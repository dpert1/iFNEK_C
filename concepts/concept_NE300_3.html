NE 300 — Fundamentals of Nuclear Engineering

Concept 3: Photon Interactions with Matter

Answer:

  • Photoelectric effect: Photon is completely absorbed by an atom and ejects a bound electron. Dominant at low photon energies (<0.1 MeV).
  • Compton scattering: Photon scatters off a loosely bound electron, transferring part of its energy. Dominant at intermediate energies (0.1–10 MeV).
  • Pair production: Photon converts into an electron-positron pair near a nucleus. Requires photon energy >1.022 MeV to create the pair of particles; dominant at high energies.
