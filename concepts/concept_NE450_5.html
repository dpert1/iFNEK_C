NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Concept 5: Fractionation

Answer:

Fractionation is the physical separation of fission products from each other and from unfissioned nuclear material during and after a nuclear detonation. Because different fission products have different volatilities and physical/chemical properties, they condense and attach to fallout particles at different rates as the fireball cools. The result is that the ratio of radionuclides in fallout particles is not the same as the original fission product yield distribution — some isotopes are enriched on particles while others are depleted, complicating dose assessment and forensic analysis of nuclear fallout.
