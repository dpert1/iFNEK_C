NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Concept 1: Tamper vs. Reflector — Yield Advantage

Answer:

A tamper (e.g., natural uranium or tungsten surrounding the pit) is more advantageous for yield. A reflector scatters neutrons back to the core. A tamper provides inertial resistance that delays hydrodynamic disassembly of the core. By keeping the supercritical assembly together longer, the tamper allows more fission generations to occur before the device kinetically disassembles, substantially increasing total energy yield beyond the energy gains due to scattered neutrons from a reflector.
