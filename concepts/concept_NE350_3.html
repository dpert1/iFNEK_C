NE 350 — Radiological Engineering Design

Concept 3: Nuclear Engineering Design Process

Answer:

The nuclear engineering design process follows iterative steps: (1) Define the problem — establish requirements, constraints, and safety criteria; (2) Conceptual design — identify candidate solutions; (3) Analysis and modeling — apply radiation transport, thermal-hydraulic, and structural analyses; (4) Optimize and select — balance performance, safety, cost, and regulatory compliance; (5) Prototype and test — validate models against experimental data; (6) Iterate — refine based on test results. Safety and ALARA principles are integrated at every step.
