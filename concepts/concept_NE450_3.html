NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Concept 3: Importance of Fusion Boosting

Answer:

Fusion boosting introduces a small quantity of D-T gas into the fissile pit. Near peak compression, the fission chain reaction heats the gas sufficiently to initiate D-T fusion. The 14.1-MeV fusion neutrons add to the neutron population at the critical moment, significantly increasing yield without increasing the mass of fissile material — allowing higher yields in a smaller, lighter device.
