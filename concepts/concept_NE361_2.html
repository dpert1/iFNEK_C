Concept 2: Approximations to the Neutron Transport Equation

Answer:

  • Diffusion theory: Replaces the angular flux with a scalar flux using Fick's Law. Valid where scattering dominates and gradients are mild. Used in most reactor physics codes.
  • SN (Discrete Ordinates): Discretizes the angular variable into a finite set of directions. More accurate near boundaries and strong absorbers than diffusion.
  • PN (Spherical Harmonics): Expands the angular flux in Legendre polynomials. P₁ approximation reduces to diffusion theory; higher orders improve accuracy.
