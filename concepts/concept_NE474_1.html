NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Concept 1: Secular vs. Transient Equilibrium

Answer:

  • Secular equilibrium: Occurs when the parent half-life is much greater than the daughter's (t½,parent ≫ t½,daughter). The parent activity is essentially constant on short timescales, and the daughter activity grows until it equals the parent activity. Daughter activity ≈ parent activity at equilibrium.
  • Transient equilibrium: Occurs when the parent half-life is moderately longer than the daughter's (t½,parent > t½,daughter, but not by orders of magnitude). The daughter activity grows to a level slightly higher than the parent and then decays at the parent's rate. Daughter activity > parent activity at equilibrium.
