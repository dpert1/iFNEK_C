Concept 7: PWR and BWR Major Components

PWR (Pressurized Water Reactor):

  • Core: Contains fuel, control rods, and coolant under high pressure (~2000 psi). Generates ~3500 MW\(_{th}\)
  • Pressurizer: Maintains system pressure via heaters and spray nozzles.
  • Steam Generator: Heat exchanger between primary to secondary loops; secondary loop produces steam to drive turbine.
  • Reactor coolant pumps: Large electrically driven pumps (~5 MW) that drive coolant through primary system.
  • Turbine and Generator: Series of blades that converts high-pressure steam to low-pressure steam, exerting torque to convert mechanical energy to electrical energy.
  • Condenser: Heat exchanger that condenses low-pressure steam in secondary loop to liquid water.

BWR (Boiling Water Reactor):

  • Core: Contains fuel, control rods, and coolant at ~1000 psi. Coolant boils directly in the core. Steam separators/dryers lie atop core.
  • Turbine and Generator: Series of blades that converts high-pressure steam to low-pressure steam, exerting torque to convert mechanical energy to electrical energy.
  • Condenser: Heat exchanger that condenses low-pressure steam to liquid water.
  • Reactor coolant pumps: Large electrically driven pumps (~5 MW) that drive coolant through primary system.
