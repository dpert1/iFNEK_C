Concept 6: Neutron Life Cycle & Four Factor Formula

Life Cycle:

A neutron is born at ~2 MeV from fission. It slows via scattering with the moderator (fast → epithermal → thermal). At thermal energies (~0.025 eV) it diffuses until absorbed, most likely in the fuel where it causes another fission.

Four Factor Formula (\(k_\infty = \varepsilon p f \eta \)):

  • ε (epsilon): Fast fission factor — accounts for fissions from fast neutrons before thermalization.
  • p: Resonance escape probability — fraction of neutrons that avoid resonance absorption while slowing down.
  • f: Thermal utilization factor — fraction of thermal neutron absorptions that occur in the fuel.
  • η (eta): Thermal fission factor — neutrons produced per neutron absorbed in fuel.
