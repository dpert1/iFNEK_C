NE 452 — Instrumentation and Shielding

Concept 1: Three Major Gamma Ray Interactions

Answer:

  • Photoelectric effect: Photon is completely absorbed by a bound electron, which is ejected. Dominant at low energies (<0.1 MeV); cross section scales as \(\frac{Z^{4-5}}{E_{\gamma}^{3.5}}\).
  • Compton scattering: Partial energy transfer to a free or loosely bound electron; scattered photon continues at reduced energy and deflected angle. Dominant at intermediate energies (0.1–10 MeV); scales linearly with Z.
  • Pair production: Photon converts to an electron-positron pair in the Coulomb field of a nucleus. Requires Eγ > 1.022 MeV; dominant at high energies (>5–10 MeV); scales as Z².
