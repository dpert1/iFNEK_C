NE 361 — Computational Design in Nuclear Engineering

Concept 4: Benefits of CAD and CAM

Answer:

  • Precision and accuracy: CAD enables exact geometric modeling, reducing human drafting error and allowing tight tolerances in complex nuclear component designs.
  • Iterative design efficiency: Modifications to geometry propagate automatically throughout the model, enabling rapid design iterations without redrawing.
  • Direct manufacturing integration: CAM translates CAD geometry directly into machine tool paths, reducing interpretation errors between design and fabrication and enabling automation of complex machining operations.
