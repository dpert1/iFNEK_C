NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Concept 4: Advantage of a U-238 Tamper on the Fusion Secondary

Answer:

The 14.1-MeV neutrons produced by D-T fusion in the secondary are above the fission threshold of U-238 (~1 MeV). A U-238 (natural or depleted uranium) tamper surrounding the secondary undergoes fast fission from these high-energy neutrons, releasing additional energy and significantly increasing total weapon yield beyond what the primary fission and secondary fusion stages alone produce. This energy contribution can account for a substantial fraction of a thermonuclear weapon's total yield.
