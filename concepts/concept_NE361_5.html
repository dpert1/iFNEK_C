NE 361 — Computational Design in Nuclear Engineering

Concept 5: MCNP Random Walk Process

Answer:

MCNP simulates individual particle histories using the Monte Carlo method. Each particle is born at a source point; random numbers are used at each step to sample: (1) the distance to the next collision from the mean free path distribution, (2) the type of interaction at the collision site (using cross section data), and (3) the post-collision energy and direction. This "random walk" continues until the particle is absorbed, exits the geometry, or falls below an energy cutoff. Tallying outcomes across millions of particle histories yields statistically converged estimates of flux, dose, or k-effective.
