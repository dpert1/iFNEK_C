NE 355 — Nuclear Reactor Engineering

Concept 2: Delayed Neutrons and Reactor Control

Answer:

Origin: A small fraction (~0.65% for U-235) of neutrons are not emitted instantly following fission. Instead, certain fission fragments (delayed neutron precursors) undergo beta decay and then emit neutrons, with half-lives ranging from ~0.2 to ~55 seconds.

Importance: Without delayed neutrons, the mean neutron generation time would be ~10⁻⁵ s, making reactor control mechanically impossible. Delayed neutrons extend the effective generation time to ~0.1 s, allowing control rods and mechanical systems to respond in time to regulate reactor power.
