NE 452 — Instrumentation and Shielding

Concept 5: Detector Response Function vs. Detector Size

Answer:

For a small detector, incident photons are likely to undergo only one interaction (e.g., a single Compton scatter) before escaping — the spectrum shows a Compton continuum with little or no full-energy peak. As detector size increases, the probability of secondary photons (scattered gammas, annihilation photons) also being absorbed within the detector increases. A large detector approaches full-energy peak dominance, where most photon energy is fully deposited, yielding a sharp photopeak for monoenergetic incident gamma rays. The response function thus transitions from continuum-dominated to photopeak-dominated as detector volume increases.
