NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Concept 5: Absorbed, Equivalent, and Effective Dose

Answer:

  • Absorbed dose (D): Energy deposited per unit mass of tissue [Gy = J/kg]. A purely physical quantity — does not account for radiation type or tissue sensitivity.
  • Equivalent dose (H): Absorbed dose weighted by the radiation weighting factor (wR), which accounts for the relative biological effectiveness of the radiation type. \(H = w_R \cdot D\) [Sv]. Gamma/X-ray: wR = 1; alpha: wR = 20.
  • Effective dose (E): Equivalent dose further weighted by tissue weighting factors (WT), accounting for the varying radiosensitivity of different organs. \(E = \sum w_T \cdot H_T\) [Sv]. Used for stochastic risk assessment across the whole body.
