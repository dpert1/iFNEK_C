NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Concept 3: Three Qualities of Stochastic Effects

Answer:

  • No threshold: Any dose, however small, is assumed to carry some probability of effect (linear no-threshold model).
  • Probability increases with dose: Higher dose increases the likelihood of the effect occurring, not its severity.
  • Not individually attributable (Random): The effect (e.g., cancer) is clinically indistinguishable from the same effect caused by non-radiological factors; only detectable statistically in large populations.
