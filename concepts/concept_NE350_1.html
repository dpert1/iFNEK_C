NE 350 — Radiological Engineering Design

Concept 1: Uses of Radioisotopes in Industry

Answer:

  • Medical imaging & treatment: Tc-99m for diagnostic scans; I-131 for thyroid cancer treatment; Co-60 for radiation therapy.
  • Industrial radiography: Ir-192 and Co-60 used to inspect welds and structural integrity via gamma imaging.
  • Gauging and measurement: Am-241 in smoke detectors; radioactive sources for density, level, and thickness gauges in manufacturing.
  • Tracer studies: Radioactive tracers used to track fluid flow, pipeline leaks, and environmental transport.
  • Food irradiation: Gamma sources used to sterilize food and medical equipment.
