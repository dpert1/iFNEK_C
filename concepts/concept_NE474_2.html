NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Concept 2: Three Qualities of Deterministic (Non-Stochastic) Effects

Answer:

  • Threshold dose: Effects only occur above a minimum dose; below the threshold, no effect is observed.
  • Severity increases with dose: Above the threshold, the severity of the effect (not just probability) increases as dose increases.
  • Causality: The effect has a recognized clinical presentation that can be linked to the radiation exposure (e.g., erythema, acute radiation syndrome).
