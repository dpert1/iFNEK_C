NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Concept 2: Two Phenomena That Shutdown the Chain Reaction

Answer:

  • Kinetic disassembly: The enormous energy release from the chain reaction heats and pressurizes the fissile material, causing it to expand and fly apart. As the material disperses, the density drops below critical, and the chain reaction terminates. This is the primary shutdown mechanism.
  • Burnup: As the fuel is consumed through the fission process, the criticality decreases, eventually dropping below one, so the chain reaction dies out.
