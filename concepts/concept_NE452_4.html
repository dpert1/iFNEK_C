NE 452 — Instrumentation and Shielding

Concept 4: Semiconductor Detector Operation

Answer:

Radiation ionizes the semiconductor material (typically germanium or silicon), creating electron-hole pairs. The energy required per pair (~3 eV in Ge) is much lower than in gas detectors (~30 eV) or scintillators, resulting in far more charge carriers per unit energy and therefore superior energy resolution. A reverse-biased p-n junction sweeps carriers apart before recombination, producing a current pulse. HPGe (high-purity germanium) detectors require cryogenic cooling to reduce thermally generated leakage current.
