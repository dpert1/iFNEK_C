NE 350 — Radiological Engineering Design

Concept 2: Gas-Filled Detector Pulse Size vs. Voltage Regions

Answer:

  • Recombination region: Low voltage; ions recombine before reaching electrodes. Pulse size increases with voltage.
  • Ionization chamber region: All primary ion pairs collected; pulse proportional to energy deposited. Alpha and beta distinguishable.
  • Proportional region: Gas multiplication amplifies signal; pulse still proportional to initial ionization. Can distinguish radiation types.
  • Limited proportionality: Space charge effects distort proportionality; transition zone.
  • Geiger-Mueller (GM) region: Full avalanche discharge; all pulses same size regardless of radiation type or energy. Cannot distinguish.
  • Continuous discharge: Voltage too high; continuous ionization, detector damaged.
