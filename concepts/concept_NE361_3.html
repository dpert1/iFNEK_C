Concept 3: Depletion Curve Dip at Constant Power

Answer:

At the start of depletion, Xe-135 and Sm-149 (fission product poisons) are not yet present. Running at constant power causes these poisons to build up rapidly in the first few days — Xe-135 reaches equilibrium in ~40–50 hours, Sm-149 in several days. To maintain constant power as these absorbers accumulate, reactivity (and thus k) must decrease sharply. This appears as a dip in the k-effective curve in the first days. After equilibrium is reached, the curve flattens and then decreases linearly as fuel burns up.
