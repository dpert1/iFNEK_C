NE 355 — Nuclear Reactor Engineering

Concept 5: Diffusion as Applied to Neutrons

Answer:

Neutron diffusion applies Fick's Law: neutrons migrate from regions of high flux (high concentration) to regions of low flux, analogous to heat or chemical diffusion. The net neutron current density is expressed \(\vec{J} = -D \vec{\nabla} \phi\), where \(D\) is the diffusion coefficient and \(\phi\) is the scalar flux. Combined with the neutron balance equation, this yields the Neutron Diffusion Equation—a simplified version of the Neutron Transport Equation with no angular dependence. It is most applicable when 1) scattering dominates over absorption, 2) scattering is isotropic, and 3) not near boundaries with vacuum or strong absorbers.
